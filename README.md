# Hi, I'm Rajesh Bhandari 👋

### Civil Engineering Graduate | Aspiring Structural & Earthquake Engineer

I am a **Civil Engineering graduate from Pulchowk Campus, Institute of Engineering (IOE), Tribhuvan University, Nepal**, currently gaining professional experience at **Miyamoto International**, where I work with **reinforced concrete and steel structures**, structural analysis, assessment, retrofit, and structural connections.

I am particularly interested in understanding **how structures behave under extreme loading, how existing structures can be assessed and strengthened, and how computational methods can be used to improve structural engineering practice**.
